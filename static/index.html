      How this is calculated

      Each day's saving is what that day's usage would have cost on your tariff with no solar or battery, minus what you actually paid, including credits and feed-in. Purchases are entered in the config file. The projection extends the last 12 months of savings, assuming electricity prices (and so the savings) rise by a set percentage each year: price_rise_percent in the [system] section of the config file, 3 by default.

        Cars

        How this is calculated

        Each day is priced twice on your tariff: what you actually paid, and what the same usage would have cost with no solar or battery. The difference is the saving. The supply charge is in both, so the saving is the power you did not buy, plus feed-in and any credits. Where a real bill covers the day (GloBird's own, or a past bill you listed in the config, spread evenly over its days), the bill's figures are used instead (the day's bill, feed-in, credits and grid kWh), and the saving is that day's no-solar cost less the bill. The rest are our estimate. Days with no data are left out.

        Include cars adds the fuel the cars saved. For each day that is the petrol the same driving would have cost, less what charging the cars would have cost from the grid with no solar or battery. The system's own share of the car savings is already in the normal saving, because the cars are part of the house load, so adding the fuel saved never counts anything twice. A day where the cars were only charged shows a negative number.
